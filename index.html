About

How DexVacs helps everyone involved

We built this platform to cut down on missed shots, prevent clinics from running out of vaccines, and give families a dependable way to keep track of their children's health history.

For parents

Manage records for all your children in one place. Follow recommended timelines for BCG, OPV, Pentavalent, Rotavirus, and Measles, book visits, and print official certificates when you need them.

Register as parent
For hospitals & clinics

Manage your daily patient queue easily, check IDs on arrival, and log doses. Marking a dose as given updates the patient's record and your inventory counts automatically.

Register hospital
For administrators

Oversee the vaccine catalog, review and approve new facilities, track inventory across the network, and make sure every clinic meets standards.

How it works

Getting started is simple

1. Create a profile

Sign up as a parent and add your child's basic details and date of birth.

2. Book an appointment

Pick a nearby hospital, select the required vaccine, and choose a date.

3. Visit the clinic

Show up for the appointment. The clinic staff will verify and administer the dose.

4. Get your certificate

The record updates instantly, and you can download or print the digital certificate.

Why DexVacs

Why move away from paper cards?

Paper cards easily get lost, and separate clinic logs don't talk to each other. DexVacs keeps everything in one secure, shared system.

Paper records vs DexVacs

FAQs

Common questions

Find answers about appointments, child profiles, and certificates.

How do I register my child and book an appointment?

Create a parent account on the registration page. Under the children section, add your child's profile, then use the booking tool to pick a hospital, vaccine, and date.

How do I get my child's vaccination certificate?

Once the clinic marks a dose as completed, it shows up in your child's history. Click "View Certificate" to open and print it whenever you need it.

Can I book appointments for past dates?

No, the calendar blocks past dates. When a clinic finishes an appointment, the system automatically subtracts one dose from their local inventory.

How can hospitals join the platform?

Hospitals can sign up through the hospital registration page. An administrator will review and approve the facility before it appears in the booking directory.

What happens if we switch to a different hospital?

Your records are stored centrally, so any approved network hospital can view past doses and keep track of future shots without starting over.
